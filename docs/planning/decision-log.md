| DEC-001 | Resolved | `otherones/small-moves` is the sole implementation and documentation target. Legacy Principal Emergency Fund repositories are out of scope and will be deprecated; they are not deployment or requirements sources. | Product-owner direction, 2026-07-28 | Repository strategy, history, deployment |
| DEC-002 | Resolved | Model one Firebase UID as one person identity. School access is granted through explicit, auditable school memberships with one or more roles; organization-admin capabilities are separate from school membership. A person may hold different roles across schools or both Principal and Additional Contact roles for one school, with capabilities evaluated for the active school and the union of assigned roles. An Additional Contact receives an invite, can submit requests, manage only requests they initiated, and add/change payout information for those requests at an allowed payout stage. An Additional Contact cannot edit school details, manage contacts or permissions, sign/renew the MOU, or change roles. The Principal may manage the school’s Additional Contact list and retains school, personnel, and MOU authority. Role assignment/elevation, including Contact-to-Principal, requires an authorized system administrator and never occurs through self-service. Holding a Principal role requires that person’s current principal/school/year/version MOU signature. | Product-owner direction (submitted as DEC-020 context; reconciled to the account/role decision), 2026-07-28 | Identity, permissions, contacts, requests, payout, MOU, audit |
| DEC-003 | Resolved | Firebase Authentication is the credential, password-reset, email-verification, session, and MFA provider. Use Firebase Authentication with Identity Platform for TOTP. This does not authorize a custom credential backend, portal-issued backup codes, or a custom administrator password/MFA reset or bypass.
